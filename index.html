My story

Where I'm coming from.

Growing up, there was nothing special about my life. Average kid, average shape, average routine; school, home, study, repeat. I wasn't working, I wasn't building anything, I wasn't really thinking about any of it either. That was just life. Then things started shifting. I fell into the trap a lot of guys fall into, the 4am alarms, cold showers, cutting out friends, bland food, absolutist mode. I thought that was discipline. Eventually I hit a wall. The performance wore off and I had nothing real to show for it. The real turning point was when my family opened a Lebanese bakery, Kaaketna. I went from a part-time sales associate to running operations; scheduling, finances, marketing, HR, while staying in school full time. It was the first time I was actually building something. But I also completely quit the gym and let my health go. I learned the hard way that going all-in on one thing while letting everything else collapse isn't discipline, it's just a different kind of imbalance. That lesson is what drives everything I do now. I'm 21, I'm in school, I'm interning in the corporate world, I'm back in the gym, and I'm still present in my personal life. Not because I cracked some code, but because I've already made most of the mistakes so you don't have to.
